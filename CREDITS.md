# Credits

Liftoff Light derives from [tcballard/omarchy-theme-liftoff](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-theme-liftoff). Original Liftoff palette authored for Tom Ballard. The original MIT licence and copyright notice are retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE); the licence covers theme configuration and documentation, not a new rights grant over the wallpapers.

The light palette is maintained by Fail-Safe and adapts the original colours to near-white surfaces and darker text/accents. The two included wallpapers are copied unchanged from the locally installed Liftoff theme. Detailed source and enhancement records remain in the [upstream credits](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-theme-liftoff/blob/main/CREDITS.md).

## backgrounds/01-liftoff.png

Image credit: SpaceX. Source supplied by Tom Ballard: https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2104654998034690329

The original photograph was supplied by Tom Ballard on 29 September 2026. On 30 September 2026, Tom confirmed that the image was published by SpaceX and released into the public domain. This rights statement is based on the repository author's confirmation; an independent image-specific licence verification was not available from the linked X post. No specific Creative Commons licence is asserted.

The 8192 × 4608 wallpaper is Tom's approved two-pass contextual AI enhancement. Fine detail is reconstructed; the dimensions describe the assembled export resolution, not a single native AI generation. The original framing and 16:9 aspect ratio are preserved.

## backgrounds/03-liftoff-sunset.png

A painterly AI reinterpretation of the launchpad photograph, added at Tom Ballard's request. The source photograph's rights statement above applies to the source of this variant. The style-reference image is not included.

The 8192 × 4608 PNG preserves the original wallpaper's 16:9 proportions. Painted detail is reconstructed rather than recovered from the photograph. [Upstream enhancement record](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-theme-liftoff/blob/main/docs/sunset-enhancement.md).
